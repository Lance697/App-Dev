# My Favorite Series

## Stranger Things

Stranger Things is one of my favorite series. It is about a group of friends who experience strange things in their town. The story has mystery, adventure, and a little bit of horror.

### Why I Like It

I like this series because the story is interesting and never gets boring. I also enjoy watching the characters and how they help each other.

- The story is interesting.
- The characters are funny and cool.
- There are many exciting scenes.
- Every episode makes me want to watch the next one.

### My Favorite Character

**Dustin Henderson**

Dustin is my favorite because he is funny and smart. He also cares about his friends and always tries to help them.

### My Favorite Season

**Season 4**

I like Season 4 because there are many exciting scenes and unexpected events. It is also one of the seasons that I enjoyed watching the most.

### My Rating

**9/10**

> I really enjoy watching Stranger Things because it has a good story and interesting characters.

### Conclusion

Overall, Stranger Things is a series that I would watch again. I recommend it to people who like mystery, adventure, and horror stories.
